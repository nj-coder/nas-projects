Description
The Little Bunny Learning Tower is a thoughtfully designed 3-in-1 piece that helps your little one safely take part in everyday moments at home. From standing at the right height to join you in the kitchen or at the sink to transforming into a table, chair, and chalkboard for play and creativity, it is designed to support practical life, growing independence, and longer-term everyday use. With a modern, family-friendly design and thoughtful 2026 upgrades for added safety and stability, it brings together function, comfort, and lasting value in one beautiful piece.

## Frequently asked questions
![Bunny-shaped 3-in-1 Montessori learning tower in a children's playroom, eco-friendly wood with a steadier 2026 updated design](https://thelittlebunny.com.au/cdn/shop/files/montessori-learning-tower-bunny-hero-banner.jpg?v=1782578484&width=1280)
What age is it suitable for?
The Little Bunny Learning Tower is made for curious little explorers **from 1 to 5 years old**. Some children are **ready to hop on as early as 10 months.**
_**** Please note: Always under the attentive supervision of an adult.**_
Will it fit my kitchen?
**W 40 cm × L 36 cm × H 86 cm**. Product weight: **7.6 kg**
The tower’s height is measured **from the floor to the ears** , making it a perfect match for almost all standard Australian kitchen benches. It’s designed to give your child the right reach while staying compact enough to **save space in your home**.
Is it safety-tested?
Yes! Our towers are **lab-tested to AS/NZS ISO 8124** (Eurofins certified) for safety and durability.
What coating/finish is used?
Each tower is finished with a gentle, water-based coating that’s completely non-toxic. It's safe for little hands (and those curious mouths!) while keeping the wood strong and beautiful for everyday adventures.
How easy is assembly?
Super easy! Each tower comes with clear step-by-step instructions, and most parents have it ready in just 10–20 minutes.
How does it help my child?
**Imagine this:** your little one climbs safely up their learning tower, big smile on their face, ready to be part of the kitchen fun, whether it’s helping mix ingredients or simply watching you cook. 
When mealtime is over, the tower quickly transforms into a cozy table and chair set for snacks, colouring, or play. 
Built right in is a **chalkboard panel** , perfect for doodles and first letters. If you’d like to add even more fun, our **magnetic whiteboard (sold separately)** makes it a space for learning games and creative play. 
_**Beyond the features, what families love most is how the tower encourages independence, builds confidence, and turns everyday moments like cooking, drawing, and snacking into opportunities to grow and connect together.**_
