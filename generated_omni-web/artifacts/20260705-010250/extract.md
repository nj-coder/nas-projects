## Description
The Ninja Crispi® Pro Glass Countertop Air Fryer brings 2050W of cooking power to your countertop. Included is a 5.7L CleanCrisp™ Glassware that serves up to 10 and a 2.3L CleanCrisp™ container for everyday meals. Designed for non-toxic cooking and storing with thermal shock resistant glass. Built compatible with multiple glassware sizes—perfect for crowds, leftovers, and party favourites.
**Key Features**
**Family-Sized Cooking System**  
5.7L and 2.3L glassware, storage lids, crisper plates, and modular base for any occasion.
**Powerful Cooking System**   
2050-watt air fryer that can cook a 3.4kg chicken, feeding up to 10 people.
**Versatile Capacity**  
Modular base adjusts to fit 2.3L, 3.8L, and 5.7L CleanCrisp™ Glassware for everyday or family-sized meals.
**Nontoxic & Durable**  
CleanCrisp™ glassware is designed for non-toxic cooking and storing. CleanCrisp™ glassware is also thermal shock resistant—go from frozen to crispy in minutes.
**Customisable 6-in-1 Functions  
** Max Crisp, Air Fry, Bake/Prove, Roast, Recrisp, and Dehydrate.
**Temperature Control**  
Cook accurately and consistently with precise temperature control for perfect results every time.
**Leakproof Storage Lids**  
Snap-lock lids keep meals fresh and prevent spills—ideal for prepping or storing leftovers.
**Elevated Design**  
A modern glass countertop system that allows you to keep an eye on apps, mains, and desserts as they cook.
**Fixed Heat Protection**  
Permanently fixed heat-safe feet so you can safely set glassware on granite, laminate, butcher block, and marble.
