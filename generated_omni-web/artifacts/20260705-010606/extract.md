####  Frequently Asked Questions 
How does the NoCo® nonstick technology work? 
This technology is the very first of its kind, setting a new standard in cookware. It uses ultra-durable titanium to create an interior surface that is virtually indestructible. Mimicking the Lotus Effect (the phenomenon of water droplets forming on leaves, rather than being absorbed), the pattern pressed into the titanium makes the interior naturally hydrophobic, which in turn creates a nonstick effect. The surface simultaneously sears (really well, we might add), while the sunken pockets create airflow and a nonstick experience.
How is the Titanium Pro Cookware Set different from your ceramic nonstick Cookware Set? 
Our original Cookware Set has a ceramic nonstick coating that is ultra slippery. The Titanium Pro Cookware Set doesn’t have a coating; the pressed interior surface is what gives it a nonstick effect. A key difference between cooking with our NoCo® technology versus ceramic nonstick is that food needs to cook through and self-release to achieve the full nonstick effect. It’s pretty genius, actually! 
Once the food has had sufficient contact with the heat, it will release itself to let you know it's ready to be flipped or plated. 
Is the Titanium Pro Cookware Set non-toxic? 
Yes. Our patented NoCo® technology achieves a nonstick experience through engineered titanium rather than chemical coatings. Our Titanium Pro cookware is made without PFAS (otherwise known as Forever Chemicals).
How does the Titanium Pro Cookware Set compare to other titanium nonstick technology? 
Our interior pattern and patented technology is actually totally different from others on the market. While it may look similar, this is the first pan with a nonstick experience that is constructed, not coated. Other patterned interiors don't utilize this technology and often coat their patterns with PFAS. We worked for years on this patented technology to be able to create a pan that lasts a lifetime without Forever Chemicals.
