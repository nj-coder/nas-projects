## Frequently Asked Questions
How quickly can I switch this from a learning tower to a table and chair set? 
The conversion takes just a few seconds. The side clip-lock mechanism is designed with busy families in mind, allowing you to switch easily from kitchen helper mode to snack or activity time. This convenient **2-in-1 design** is ideal for smaller kitchens where making the most of every square metre matters.
Is the Birch plywood used in the Convertible Tower durable enough for daily spills in the kitchen? 
Yes. The Convertible Learning Tower is made from **premium European birch plywood** and sealed with a **non-toxic varnish**. It is designed to be wiped clean using a lightly damp microfibre cloth. Unlike lower-grade MDF alternatives that can swell or weaken when exposed to moisture, this tower is built to handle the realities of daily family life.
My toddler is only 18 months old; will they be safe from falling out of the back? 
Safety is central to the design. The tower features a stable standing platform and protective side rails to help keep your child supported. For younger toddlers, adult supervision is always recommended. Many parents find that tower mode provides a comfortable, enclosed space where little ones can observe or participate safely during meal preparation.
Will the table mode fit two toddlers, or is it just for one? 
The table and bench seat are designed to comfortably accommodate **one toddler** for meals, drawing, or quiet play. For families with multiple children, we recommend exploring our wider Toypark range of kids’ tables and chairs to create a larger shared play or activity space.
Does it come with any accessories like a drawing board? 
This particular model focuses on its **2-in-1 convertible functionality**. However, it pairs beautifully with our Toypark Kids Aprons and Stainless Steel Cooking Sets. If you are looking for a learning tower with an integrated drawing board, we recommend exploring the **JALA series** within the My Duckling collection.
What is the height of the platform? Will it work with my standard Australian kitchen bench? 
The standing platform is positioned **50 cm from the floor** , bringing your child to an ideal working height for a standard **90 cm Australian kitchen bench**. This makes it well suited for practical life activities such as washing vegetables, mixing ingredients, or helping with simple food preparation.
