Product Description 
**Scandinavian-Inspired Design for Growing Independence & Creative Play**
The So Wood Convertible Kitchen Tower is thoughtfully designed to grow with your child — transforming everyday routines into meaningful learning moments.
Crafted with clean Scandinavian-inspired lines, this elegant piece blends seamlessly into modern homes while helping your little one safely reach counter height. Whether they’re washing hands, helping prepare meals, or exploring new skills, the sturdy elevated platform encourages independence, confidence, and family connection.
What makes it truly special? In just seconds, the tower converts into a play desk and chair — creating a dedicated space for drawing, reading, snacks, and creative play. One beautifully designed piece, multiple purposes, and no clutter.
* * *
##### Dimensions
  * **Overall height:** 88 cm (34.64")
  * **Width:** 40 cm (15.74")
  * **First step:** 37.5 × 14 cm (14.76" × 5.51")
  * **Step height from floor:** 25.5 cm (10.03")
  * **Platform:** 37.5 × 28 cm (14.76" × 11.02")
  * **Platform height from floor:** 45.5 cm (17.91")
